How to read this chart

Bars show the projected likelihood of retirement within the next five years by age group. Higher bars in the older brackets signal clusters of at-risk roles and potential knowledge loss that should be addressed through succession planning and knowledge-transfer programs.

High-risk groups (55+) The data indicates that nearly one-third of the workforce (28%) is entering a high-risk phase for departure. This group doesn't just represent "headcount"; they are likely the primary holders of legacy knowledge, complex client relationships, and specialized technical expertise.

The organization should treat this 28% figure as a KPI for Risk Management. Succession planning should no longer be a quarterly HR conversation but a core business strategy to ensure that the projected retirement rates do not result in a loss of competitive advantage.

Finance and Admin stand out with near gender parity, reflecting strong balance in representation. Commercial and Operations teams also show healthy diversity, indicating consistent progress across core business functions. In contrast, Technical Services remains more male-dominated, signaling the need for focused initiatives such as inclusive hiring practices, mentorship programs, and career development pathways aimed at increasing female representation. By closely monitoring these trends, we can implement data-driven strategies that promote equity, strengthen collaboration, and ensure all departments benefit from a diverse range of perspectives.
